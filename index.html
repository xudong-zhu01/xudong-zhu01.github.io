Research

My doctoral thesis is Strategy-Making in a Fragmented State: Security Policy, Defence Policy, and Grand Strategy in Sino-German Cooperation (1928–1938), supervised by Prof. Dr. Thomas Weber and Dr. Andrew Dilley. I am also writing Das politische System Chinas with Dr. Björn Alexander Düben, under contract with Nomos.

Applied History & Statecraft

  • Statecraft and grand-strategy frameworks applied to current geopolitical questions.
  • Comparative Realpolitik: power, interests, and constraints across cases.

Global Security & Governance
