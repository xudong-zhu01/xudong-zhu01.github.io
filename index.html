Doctoral Research

Strategy-Making in a Fragmented State: Security Policy, Defence Policy, and Grand Strategy in Sino-German Cooperation, 1928–1938

The thesis uses Sino-German military cooperation between 1928 and 1938 to ask what constitutes security policy, defence policy, and grand strategy, and how they are formulated when authority over foreign and military affairs is divided. Its questions reach beyond the China case to strategy-making in any fragmented state.
