Teaching

University of Aberdeen

HI2520: Global Empire in the Long Nineteenth Century

University of Aberdeen · 2025–26 Teaching Assistant

The long nineteenth century saw dramatic rises and falls in power systems bringing together a range of peoples and territories. This course examines this phenomenon which, for better or worse, made the modern world.

Role

  • Weekly undergraduate tutorials on primary and secondary sources.
  • Guidance on historical argument and academic writing.

HI1022: Europe in the Twentieth Century

University of Aberdeen · 2025–26 Teaching Assistant

European history across the twentieth century, with emphasis on its major political, social, and cultural transformations.

Role

  • Weekly undergraduate tutorials and source analysis.
  • Feedback on essays and individual consultations.

Earlier Teaching

From Washington to Peking: History of the Early Interactions Between the US and China

Heidelberg Center for American Studies, Heidelberg University · Summer 2023 Seminar Instructor

Seminar on the formative period of Sino-American relations, from the late eighteenth to the early twentieth century.

Selected Sessions

  • The Empress of China and the beginning of Sino-American trade
  • Anson Burlingame and the Open Door policy
  • Chinese students in America and national renewal
  • The "Yellow Peril" and the Chinese Exclusion Act

Teaching Interests

  • Modern international history
  • Great-power strategy and statecraft
  • European integration and transatlantic relations
  • Applied history and research methods
